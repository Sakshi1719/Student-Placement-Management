Executive Project Brief & Research Summary

1. Executive Summary & Problem Context

Higher education business schools face mounting scrutiny on campus placement outcomes. However, institutional decision-makers frequently lack granular diagnostic clarity on how academic performance, strict attendance compliance, and domain specialisations interact to influence recruiter compensation offers. This analytics system bridges that critical gap.

2. Key Analytical & Econometric Findings

  • 75% Attendance Threshold Disparity: Attendance compliant students (≥75%) secure a 94.15% placement rate with mean CTC of ₹10.41 LPA, whereas attendance defaulters (<75%) drop to 49.23% placement rate and ₹8.31 LPA (Welch's t = 12.20, p < 0.001).
  • Academic Elasticity: Each 1.0 point increment in student CGPA delivers an estimated +₹0.93 LPA premium on final corporate CTC.
  • Domain Specialisation Premium: Business Analytics commands the highest starting corporate compensation (Mean: ₹11.09 LPA, Peak: ₹16.09 LPA), followed by Finance (₹10.04 LPA) and Marketing (₹9.53 LPA).
  • Prescriptive Interventions: 177 students (34.0%) were flagged in Band 4 (Critical Intervention) requiring immediate remedial support to avert placement ineligibility.

3. Strategic Administrative Recommendations

  1. Mandatory Early-Warning Trigger: Initiate academic counseling at the conclusion of Semester 1 whenever student attendance drops below 75% or CGPA falls below 6.5.
  2. Integrated Certification Tracks: Mandate high-value industry certifications (Coursera / NPTEL / Power BI / Financial Modeling) in Semester 2 to close the +₹0.34 LPA per-certification skill gap.
  3. Corporate Interview Labs: For high-CGPA students with soft-skill deficits (<6.0), conduct intensive GD-PI corporate bootcamps to prevent offer rejections at final round interviews.
